Computer Control of Mechatronics Systems – Controller Design

December 2, 2014

My favourite course during my time at UBC was MECH 467: Computer Control of Mechatronics Systems. This course was fast-paced and challenging, starting with Laplace transforms and block diagram algebra, and culminating in the integrated design of complete, multi-axis motion control systems.

In the final lab (for lab requirements click here) I generated discrete-time x and y axis position commands using trapezoidal feedrate profiling, designed low, high, and mismatched bandwidth integral-lead-lag controllers, and simulated the plant’s behaviour using MATLAB/Simulink before testing my controller and trajectory generation on a real XY table.

For a taste of controller design and comparisons, I would invite you to check out my report HERE, and a bonus document discussing pole placement controllers can be found HERE.

At the end of the lab I tested my low bandwidth integral-lead-lag controller with a custom trajectory on a real XY table.
