[Arduino] Vessel Motion Demonstrator

August 12, 2020

Why do we need to worry about wave frequencies when designing equipment for an offshore vessel?

It can be tempting to underestimate the power of the ocean, which is why I built a scale model to demonstrate how wave frequencies affect vessel roll motions. If a vessel is rocked back and forth at its resonant frequency, then motions can become bigger and bigger and there is a risk of breaking equipment, or at the very least becoming seasick.

I designed this model using AutoCad for the laser cut parts and Autodesk Inventor for the overall 3D model. I programmed two arduinos to operate the device – one to manage the stepper motor behaviour, and the other to coordinate the user inputs and motor and LED outputs.
